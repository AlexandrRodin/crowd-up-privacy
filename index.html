# Privacy Policy for Crowd Up! **Last updated:** September 7, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how **Crowd Up!** ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile game. **Developer:** QuantumPeak **Contact:** quantumpeak_support@proton.me By installing or using Crowd Up!, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. ## 1. Information We Collect Crowd Up! does not require you to create an account and does not directly request information such as your name, postal address, phone number, or email address in order to play the Game. However, the Game uses third-party software development kits (SDKs), including advertising and monetization services, which may automatically collect certain information from your device. Depending on the advertising configuration and the services available to you, this information may include: * Advertising identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID; * Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, and country or region; * IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address; * Information about advertising interactions, such as impressions, clicks, and rewarded ad views; * Information about your interaction with advertisements and the Game; * Technical information used to detect errors, prevent fraud, and maintain the security and performance of advertising services. The exact information collected may vary depending on the advertising networks enabled through Unity LevelPlay. ## 2. Advertising Crowd Up! uses **Unity LevelPlay** to provide and manage advertising. The Game may display: * Banner advertisements; * Interstitial advertisements; * Rewarded advertisements. Unity LevelPlay may use third-party advertising networks to deliver advertisements. These advertising providers may process device information, advertising identifiers, and information about ad interactions for purposes such as delivering, measuring, limiting, and improving advertising. Advertising may be personalized or contextual depending on your location, consent status, device settings, and the advertising networks used by the Game. For more information about Unity's privacy practices, please refer to Unity's applicable privacy documentation. ## 3. Unity Ads Crowd Up! may use **Unity Ads** as an advertising provider through Unity LevelPlay. Unity Ads may process information such as device identifiers, device information, advertising information, and information about interactions with advertisements. Unity's advertising SDK supports privacy frameworks including GDPR, CCPA, and COPPA where applicable. The Game may communicate applicable privacy and consent choices to the advertising SDK. For more information, please review Unity's privacy documentation. ## 4. Personalized Advertising and Consent Where required by applicable law, Crowd Up! may request your consent before allowing certain advertising partners to process information for personalized advertising. If you do not provide consent where consent is required, advertisements may still be shown, but they may be contextual or otherwise limited. You may also have privacy choices available through your device settings or through consent and privacy controls presented by the Game. For users in regions where applicable privacy laws provide additional rights, we will process personal information in accordance with those laws. ## 5. Children's Privacy Crowd Up! is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through account registration or forms. Because the Game may be available to users of different ages, advertising and privacy settings may be configured according to applicable legal requirements and the settings of the advertising services used by the Game. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us directly, please contact us at: **quantumpeak_support@proton.me** ## 6.
